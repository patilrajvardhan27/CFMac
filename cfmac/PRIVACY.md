# cfmac Privacy Statement

Version 1 · Effective October 27, 2026

cfmac runs entirely on your Mac.

- **File names and sizes only.** cfmac reads file names, sizes, dates and types to find caches and measure disk use. It never opens your documents.
- **Personal data stays off limits.** cfmac never looks inside Photos, Mail, Messages, Contacts, Calendars, Safari, Keychains or Apple apps' private data, even with Full Disk Access.
- **No sensitive permissions.** cfmac never asks for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or Focus status.
- **No network, except what you ask for.** cfmac has no accounts, analytics or tracking. It contacts the internet only when you check for or install app updates, through the Homebrew and App Store tools on your Mac. When you click **Look Up**, your browser opens a Google search containing only the item's or app's name; cfmac itself sends nothing.
- **Local storage only.** Preferences, lifetime stats, imported sounds and the last automatic clean's undo list stay in your user Library. Settings › Privacy erases them.
- **Fan helper.** If you turn on fan control, a helper runs as root. It only reads and sets fan speed, and only accepts requests from your account.
